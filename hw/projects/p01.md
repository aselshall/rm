## Evaluating nature-based infrastructure for stormwater mitigation and groundwater recharge 

Faulty members: Mewcha Gebremedhin, Ahmed Elshall and Seneshaw Tsegaye   

### Project Summary
This project evaluates the effectiveness of green infrastructure in mitigating stormwater runoff and enhancing groundwater recharge by integrating surface-water and subsurface hydrologic processes. Using StormWise modeling (ICPR), the study will simulate existing conditions and compare them with scenarios incorporating site suitable green infrastructure best management practices, such as bioretention systems, bioswales, permeable pavement, forest, and wetlands. The analysis will quantify changes in runoff volume, peak flow, groundwater recharge, and groundwater levels. Future climate conditions and sea-level-rise scenarios will also be incorporated to assess the long term performance and resilience of the selected practices. The project aims to identify effective green infrastructure strategies for integrated stormwater management and groundwater sustainability.
