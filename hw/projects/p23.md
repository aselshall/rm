## From Sketch to BIM: Student Perceptions of AI-Driven Interoperability

This study aims to explore how construction management students perceive the use of AI-based BIM interoperability tools for converting different types of design files into BIM models. During the fall 2026 semester, students will complete a hands-on assignment using the Wise BIM tool to transform sketches, CAD files, and PDF drawings into BIM models. After the activity, students may voluntarily participate in a survey about the accuracy, usability, and educational value of the generated models. The results may help guide instructional strategies for integrating AI tools into construction education.
