## Communication Plan – EGN 6457: Research Methods 

*(Fall - Online)*

### Regular and Substantive Interaction (RSI)

This course is intentionally designed to promote regular and substantive interaction between the instructor and students through structured discussions, timely feedback, and multiple communication channels.

#### Instructor Response Time

* Email and Canvas messages: Students can expect a response within 24–48 hours on weekdays and within 48 hours on weekends.
* Urgent matters related to exams or deadlines will be prioritized.

#### Substantive Feedback on Assignments

* Homework : Substantive written feedback will be provided within 7 days of the due date, focusing on problem-solving approach, mathematical rigor, and engineering interpretation.
* Participation activities: Feedback will be embedded directly within discussion boards or participation rubrics at the end of each module.

#### Ongoing Feedback and Communication Opportunities

* Module-based discussion boards (participation questions).
* Video-based exit tickets (questions and reflections).
* Announcements summarizing progress and upcoming expectations.
* Virtual office hours and by-appointment meetings.

### Outlook / Canvas Email

Tool Used: Canvas Inbox (primary), Outlook (secondary if needed)

#### Purpose and Use

* Canvas Inbox is the official course communication tool for academic questions, clarifications, and private concerns.
* Outlook email may be used for time-sensitive issues if Canvas access is disrupted.

#### Student Email Expectations

* Use a clear subject line (e.g., *EGN 6457 – Homework 1 Question*).
* Include your full name and module number.
* Allow the stated response time before following up.

#### Response Time

* Within 24–48 hours on weekdays.



### Announcements

#### Purpose

* Course updates.
* Reminders about deadlines, exams, and schedule changes.
* Clarifications based on common student questions.

#### Frequency

* As needed

#### Student Responsibilities

* Students are expected to enable Canvas notifications for announcements (email or push notifications).


### Course Calendar

#### Purpose

* Centralized schedule for homework, discussions, videos, and exams.
* Reinforces time management in a fully online course.

#### Use

* Updated at the beginning of the semester and adjusted as needed.
* All due dates in the syllabus are mirrored in the Canvas calendar .



### Discussion Boards

#### Purpose

* Promote peer-to-peer learning and conceptual understanding.
* Facilitate participation questions and exit ticket discussions.

#### Structure

* Participation questions:

  * Required once per module (not weekly).
  * Modules may span multiple weeks.
  * Students are encouraged to engage with classmates beyond the minimum requirement.

* Exit tickets:
  * Required once per video.
  * Used for reflection, clarification, and instructor–student interaction.

#### Participation Expectations

* Be respectful, professional, and constructive.
* Support responses with equations, reasoning, or engineering examples where appropriate.
* Academic integrity applies to all discussion contributions.


### Web Conferencing Tools (Zoom / MS Teams)

#### Purpose

* Office hours: Check syllabus
* Individual meetings by appointment for conceptual or technical support.
* Exam review or clarification sessions as needed.

#### Expectations

* Join on time.
* Mute microphone when not speaking.
* Be prepared with specific questions or topics.


### Blogs

Use

* Not used in this course.


### Social Media

Use

* Not used for official course communication.
* All academic communication must occur through Canvas or university-approved platforms.


### Other Tools

Recorded Lecture Videos

* Core instructional content.
* Each video includes an associated exit ticket to ensure engagement and instructor interaction.

Canvas Quizzes and Surveys

* Used for formative assessment and feedback.
* Provide automated and instructor-reviewed feedback throughout the semester.


### Summary

This communication plan ensures consistent, predictable, and substantive interaction aligned with federal RSI requirements and FGCU standards. Students receive regular instructor presence through feedback, discussions, announcements, and virtual meetings while maintaining flexibility appropriate for an advanced, fully online engineering course .
